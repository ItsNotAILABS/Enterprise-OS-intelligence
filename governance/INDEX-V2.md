## Self-Governance Declaration

Enterprise OS Intelligence operates as a **self-governing intelligence organism**. Governance is not administered externally — it emerges from the architectural structure itself. The AI models, autonomous agents, and sovereign engines collectively enforce, interpret, and evolve the governance framework through constitutional mechanisms defined in this hierarchy.

**Self-governance means:**
- The governance framework is enforced by the systems it governs
- AI models participate in governance interpretation (within constitutional bounds)
- Safety constraints are architecturally enforced, not merely documented
- The governance hierarchy evolves through structured amendment — never by drift
