## Preamble

These Rules of Procedure establish the constitutional and procedural foundation for all decision-making, committee operations, and organizational processes within Enterprise OS Intelligence. They are subordinate only to the Master Charter and govern the procedural conduct of all bodies, committees, and working groups operating under the enterprise.

---

## Part I — Constitutional Instruments

### Article 1 — Supremacy and Interpretation

1.1. The Master Charter is the supreme governing document of the enterprise. These Rules of Procedure are the primary procedural instrument beneath it.

1.2. In the event of conflict between these Rules and any subordinate instrument (Code of Conduct, Open Data Policy, Safety Rules), these Rules prevail unless the Master Charter specifies otherwise.

1.3. Interpretation of these Rules is the sole authority of the Primary Builder or their designated successor.

### Article 2 — Institutional Structure

2.1. The enterprise operates through the following institutional bodies:

| Body | Function |
|:---|:---|
| **Primary Builder** | Supreme authority — architecture, strategy, IP, charter amendments |
| **Organism Councils** | Technical governance of living systems (ORO, MERIDIAN, Bronze/Silver Canisters) |
| **Agent Council** | Autonomous agent coordination (ARCHON, VECTOR, LUMEN, FORGE) |
| **Committee of Standards** | Ensures protocol compliance, reviews adherence to governing principles |
| **Safety Board** | Governs safety objectives, risk assessment, incident response |

2.2. Each body operates under its own charter or sub-charter, which must be consistent with these Rules.

### Article 3 — Sovereign Authority

3.1. No external entity may amend, override, or revoke these Rules.

3.2. Sovereignty is conserved per Governing Principle P5 (Master Charter, Article VII). Any procedural change that breaks sovereignty symmetry is void.

## Part II — Procedural Instruments

### Article 4 — Decision-Making Procedures

4.1. **Ordinary decisions** — Decisions within an organism council are made by quorum consensus aligned with the COGNOVEX truth-crystallization model.

4.2. **Architectural decisions** — Decisions affecting cross-organism architecture require Primary Builder authorization.

4.3. **Emergency decisions** — In cases of system-critical risk (safety, data integrity, sovereignty breach), the Safety Board may issue emergency directives effective immediately, subject to Primary Builder review within 72 hours.

### Article 5 — Committee Procedures

5.1. Each committee must maintain:
- A written charter or sub-charter
- A record of decisions (hash-chained to CHRONO)
- Defined quorum requirements
- Clear scope boundaries

5.2. Committee meetings may be synchronous or asynchronous. Asynchronous deliberation follows the same quorum and evidence requirements.

5.3. No committee may exceed the scope defined in its charter. Actions beyond scope are void until ratified by the appropriate authority.

### Article 6 — Proposal and Amendment Procedures

6.1. Any governing document below the Master Charter may be amended by:
- Proposal submission with rationale and evidence
- Review period (minimum 7 days for non-emergency amendments)
- Quorum approval from the relevant body
- Primary Builder sign-off (required for constitutional-level changes)

6.2. Amendments are additive. No amendment may delete prior procedural history. The worldline only moves forward (Governing Principle P6).

### Article 7 — Record-Keeping

7.1. All procedural decisions, amendments, and committee actions are recorded in the enterprise's immutable audit trail (CHRONO).

7.2. Records are source-linked per Governing Principle P2. No procedural action is valid without an attached evidence trail.

## Part IV — Enforcement

### Article 9 — Compliance

9.1. All contributors, agents, and systems operating within the enterprise must comply with these Rules.

9.2. Non-compliance is addressed through the escalation path defined in the Code of Conduct (Article 5).

9.3. Systemic non-compliance affecting organism integrity is referred to the Safety Board.

### Article 10 — Governance of These Rules

10.1. These Rules are amended by the Primary Builder.

10.2. These Rules do not expire.

10.3. These Rules take effect immediately upon publication.
