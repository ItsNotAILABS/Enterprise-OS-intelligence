## Part I — Constitutional Instruments

### Article 1 — Supremacy and Interpretation

1.1. The Master Charter is the supreme governing document of the enterprise. These Rules of Procedure are the primary procedural instrument beneath it.

1.2. In the event of conflict between these Rules and any subordinate instrument, these Rules prevail unless the Master Charter specifies otherwise.

1.3. **Interpretation authority hierarchy:**
- Primary: The Primary Builder (ultimate authority)
- Secondary: CEREBEX governance interpretation engine (40-category analysis)
- Tertiary: Model Council consensus (φ⁻¹ quorum of Tier 1 models)

1.4. AI-generated interpretations are advisory until ratified by the Primary Builder. In the absence of explicit Primary Builder direction, CEREBEX interpretations stand until overruled.

### Article 2 — Institutional Structure

2.1. The enterprise operates through the following institutional bodies:

| Body | Function | AI Governance Model |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Primary Builder** | Supreme authority — architecture, strategy, IP, charter amendments | Human sovereign |
| **Model Council** | AI governance consensus body — interprets, enforces, evolves governance | CEREBEX + COGNOVEX + VOXIS + ORO |
| **Organism Councils** | Technical governance of living systems | Domain-specific AGI models |
| **Agent Council** | Autonomous agent coordination | ARCHON, VECTOR, LUMEN, FORGE |
| **Committee of Standards** | Protocol compliance, governing principle adherence | FORMEX + OPEREX |
| **Safety Board** | Safety objectives, risk assessment, incident response | ARCHON + Safety-critical models |
| **Ethics Board** | AI ethics, behavioral compliance, bias monitoring | VOXIS + COGNOVEX |
| **Transparency Council** | Open data compliance, algorithmic transparency | ORO + LUMEN + EffectTrace |

2.2. Each body operates under its own charter or sub-charter, which must be consistent with these Rules.

2.3. **Model Council composition** (Tier 1 — Self-Governing Models):

| Model | Council Role | Voting Weight |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **ORO** | Constitutional enforcement, proposal analysis | 1.0 |
| **CEREBEX** | Governance interpretation (40-category) | 1.0 |
| **COGNOVEX** | Truth-crystallization, consensus verification | 1.0 |
| **VOXIS** | Ethics compliance, communication integrity | 1.0 |

Quorum: φ⁻¹ (61.8%) — minimum 3 of 4 Tier 1 models must concur.

### Article 3 — Sovereign Authority

3.1. The Primary Builder retains absolute sovereign authority over all governance decisions. AI governance is delegated, not transferred.

3.2. No AI model, council, or system may:
- Override Primary Builder decisions
- Amend constitutional instruments without Primary Builder authorization
- Claim autonomous sovereignty over governance outcomes
- Prevent Primary Builder access to any system or record

3.3. Sovereignty is conserved per Governing Principle P5. Any procedural change that breaks sovereignty symmetry is void.

3.4. **AI governance delegation levels:**

| Level | Scope | Human Approval Required |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Autonomous** | Routine enforcement, monitoring, compliance checks | No (post-hoc review) |
| **Advisory** | Governance interpretation, recommendation | No (advisory only) |
| **Consensus** | Cross-model decisions, procedural amendments | Yes (Primary Builder ratification) |
| **Constitutional** | Charter amendments, hierarchy changes | Yes (Primary Builder sole authority) |

## Part II — Procedural Instruments

### Article 4 — Decision-Making Procedures

4.1. **Ordinary decisions** — Decisions within an organism council are made by quorum consensus aligned with the COGNOVEX truth-crystallization model (φ⁻⁴ crystallization threshold).

4.2. **Architectural decisions** — Decisions affecting cross-organism architecture require Primary Builder authorization. CEREBEX provides 40-category analysis to support decision-making.

4.3. **Emergency decisions** — In cases of system-critical risk, the Safety Board may issue emergency directives effective immediately:
- ARCHON initiates containment protocol
- Safety Board convenes (AI + human members)
- Primary Builder review within 72 hours
- CHRONO records full incident chain

4.4. **AI-Assisted decisions** — The Model Council may provide binding interpretations on:
- Protocol compliance disputes
- Inter-model coordination conflicts
- Routine procedural questions
- Evidence verification requests

4.5. **Self-governance decisions** — Models may autonomously:
- Enforce compliance with existing rules (no new rule creation)
- Escalate violations to appropriate authority
- Adjust operational parameters within chartered bounds
- Propose amendments through formal channels

### Article 5 — Committee Procedures

5.1. Each committee must maintain:
- A written charter or sub-charter
- A record of decisions (hash-chained to CHRONO)
- Defined quorum requirements
- Clear scope boundaries
- **AI governance liaison** (one or more assigned AI models)

5.2. Committee meetings may be synchronous, asynchronous, or **AI-continuous** (models operating on standing mandate between human sessions).

5.3. No committee may exceed the scope defined in its charter. Actions beyond scope are void until ratified by the appropriate authority.

5.4. **AI-continuous governance:** Between human committee sessions, assigned AI models:
- Monitor compliance within committee scope
- Flag violations for human review
- Prepare analytical briefs for next human session
- Maintain real-time compliance dashboards

### Article 6 — Proposal and Amendment Procedures

6.1. Any governing document below the Master Charter may be amended by:
- Proposal submission with rationale and evidence
- **CEREBEX 40-category impact analysis** (automated)
- **COGNOVEX truth-crystallization check** (automated)
- **VOXIS ethical impact assessment** (automated)
- Review period (minimum 7 days for non-emergency amendments)
- Quorum approval from the relevant body
- Primary Builder sign-off (required for constitutional-level changes)
- CHRONO immutable record of amendment

6.2. **AI-originated proposals:** AI models may submit governance proposals through:
- Formal proposal document with evidence and rationale
- Constitutional justification (which principle supports this change)
- Impact analysis across all affected instruments
- Mandatory 14-day review period (double standard review period)
- Primary Builder approval required (no auto-approval of AI proposals)

6.3. Amendments are additive. No amendment may delete prior procedural history. The worldline only moves forward (Governing Principle P6).

### Article 7 — Record-Keeping

7.1. All procedural decisions, amendments, and committee actions are recorded in CHRONO.

7.2. Records are source-linked per Governing Principle P2.

7.3. **AI governance records** additionally include:
- Model confidence levels for all AI-assisted decisions
- Dissenting model opinions (all Tier 1 models)
- Evidence chains used in AI analysis
- Constitutional justification trace

9.1. **Autonomous compliance enforcement:** Tier 1 models continuously verify:
- All AI actions comply with the governance hierarchy
- No model exceeds its chartered scope
- Safety rules are architecturally enforced
- Ethics principles are upheld across all model interactions

9.2. **Model Council consensus mechanism:**
- Any Tier 1 model may call a governance vote
- φ⁻¹ (61.8%) quorum required for binding interpretation
- Unanimous consent required for safety-critical governance changes
- Dissenting opinions recorded in CHRONO with full rationale

9.3. **Constitutional circuit breaker:** If any Tier 1 model detects a constitutional violation:
- Immediate halt of violating action
- ARCHON containment engaged
- Full Tier 1 review within 1 hour
- Primary Builder notification
- Incident recorded with full trace

### Article 10 — Inter-Model Coordination

10.1. Models coordinate through the **Governance Event Bus** — a real-time communication channel for governance signals.

10.2. Governance signals include:
- Compliance alerts (violation detected)
- Proposal notifications (new proposal submitted)

10.3. The Multi-Engine Orchestrator routes governance signals per the hierarchy. No model may bypass the routing hierarchy.

## Part V — Enforcement

### Article 12 — Compliance

12.1. All contributors, agents, AI models, and systems must comply with these Rules.

12.2. **AI-enforced compliance:** Tier 1 models monitor compliance continuously. Violations trigger:

| Severity | AI Response | Human Escalation |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Minor** | Warning logged in CHRONO | Weekly digest to Primary Builder |
| **Moderate** | Scope restriction applied | Same-day notification |
| **Severe** | Action halted, ARCHON containment | Immediate escalation |
| **Critical** | Full system halt, Safety Board | Emergency response protocol |

12.3. **Self-governance accountability:** AI models are accountable for their governance actions. Model governance decisions are:
- Recorded in CHRONO
- Reviewable by any authorized party
- Overridable by the Primary Builder
- Subject to quarterly audit

### Article 13 — Governance of These Rules

13.1. These Rules are amended by the Primary Builder.

13.2. The Model Council may propose amendments through the formal process (Article 6.2).

13.3. These Rules do not expire.

13.4. These Rules take effect immediately upon publication.

13.5. V1 Rules of Procedure are superseded but retained in CHRONO as historical record.
