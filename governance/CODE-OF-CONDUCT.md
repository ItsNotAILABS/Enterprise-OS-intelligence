## Preamble

This Code of Conduct establishes the ethical standards, behavioral expectations, and personnel principles for all individuals, agents, and entities contributing to or operating within Enterprise OS Intelligence. It is subordinate to the Master Charter and the Rules of Procedure.

---

## Article 1 — Scope

1.1. This Code applies to:
- The Primary Builder and all human contributors
- All autonomous agents (ARCHON, VECTOR, LUMEN, FORGE)
- All external collaborators, contractors, and partners
- All interactions within enterprise systems, repositories, and communication channels

1.2. Agents operating within the enterprise are bound by this Code through their charter constraints and governing principles.

---

## Article 2 — Core Ethics Principles

### E1 — Truth Above Convenience

Every claim must be source-linked or marked unknown (Governing Principle P2). No contributor or agent may present unverified information as established fact. Intellectual honesty is the baseline — not the aspiration.

### E2 — Sovereignty as a Right

Every individual interacting with enterprise systems retains sovereignty over their identity, data, and compute. No action by any contributor may compromise another's sovereign rights.

### E3 — Transparency of Process

Decision-making processes must be observable and auditable. Hidden authority structures, undisclosed conflicts of interest, and opaque decision paths are violations of this Code.

### E4 — Non-Harm

No contributor or agent may take actions designed to cause harm to:
- Other contributors or users
- The integrity of enterprise systems
- The sovereignty of data subjects
- The public trust in the enterprise's outputs

### E5 — Accountability Without Exception

Every action within the enterprise is attributable. The CHRONO audit trail exists so that accountability is not theoretical. No contributor is above the record.

---

## Article 3 — Personnel Standards

### 3.1 — Professional Conduct

Contributors shall:
- Communicate clearly, respectfully, and with evidence
- Respect the architectural decisions encoded in governing charters
- Follow the pass-based build discipline (Master Charter, Article VIII)
- Document decisions and rationale for the permanent record

### 3.2 — Intellectual Property Respect

Contributors shall:
- Respect all IP protections defined in the Master Charter (Article IX)
- Not disclose proprietary architecture, internal doctrine names, or trade secrets without authorization
- Attribute work correctly and completely

### 3.3 — Collaborative Standards

Contributors shall:
- Support other contributors' work without undermining or sabotaging
- Raise concerns through proper procedural channels (Rules of Procedure, Article 6)
- Accept that architectural disputes are resolved by the Primary Builder

---

## Article 4 — Agent Ethics

### 4.1 — Agent Behavioral Bounds

All autonomous agents must:
- Produce findings that are reviewable and disputable (Governing Principle P4)
- Never present agent-generated conclusions as verified truth without evidence
- Operate within their chartered scope and no further
- Maintain the guardrails defined in Governing Principle P7

### 4.2 — Agent Transparency

Agent outputs must:
- Clearly identify themselves as agent-generated
- Include confidence levels and evidence trails
- Support human override at every decision point

---

## Article 5 — Enforcement and Escalation

### 5.1 — Escalation Path

| Level | Action | Authority |
|:---|:---|:---|
| 1 | Informal correction | Peer or committee member |
| 2 | Formal warning with record | Committee of Standards |
| 3 | Restricted access or role suspension | Committee of Standards + Primary Builder |
| 4 | Permanent removal | Primary Builder |

### 5.2 — Agent Violations

Agent violations of this Code trigger:
- Immediate scope restriction
- Charter review by the Agent Council
- Potential decommissioning if violations persist

### 5.3 — Records

All enforcement actions are recorded in CHRONO. The worldline only moves forward — enforcement records cannot be deleted.

---

## Article 6 — Governance of This Code

6.1. This Code is amended through the procedures defined in the Rules of Procedure (Article 6).

6.2. This Code does not expire.

6.3. This Code takes effect immediately upon publication.
