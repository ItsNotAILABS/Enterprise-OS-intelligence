## Preamble

This Open Data Policy defines the enterprise's commitments, principles, and boundaries regarding data openness, public transparency, and information sharing. It is subordinate to the Master Charter, the Rules of Procedure, and the Code of Conduct.

The enterprise publishes all prior art publicly (Master Charter, Article VI). This policy formalizes the scope, methods, and limitations of that openness.

---

## Article 1 — Principles of Openness

### O1 — Public Prior Art

All research papers, architectural specifications, and theoretical foundations are published publicly as prior art. Publication establishes the intellectual record and protects the enterprise's innovation timeline.

### O2 — Verifiable Outputs

Public-facing outputs of enterprise systems (e.g., EffectTrace governance intelligence) must be verifiable by any observer. Source links, evidence trails, and truth-status labels are public by design.

### O3 — Sovereignty-Preserving Openness

Openness must not compromise:
- Individual sovereignty over personal data
- Enterprise sovereignty over proprietary implementations
- System integrity or security
- The conservation of sovereign compute (Governing Principle P5)

### O4 — Structured Transparency

Transparency is structured, not indiscriminate. The enterprise publishes what strengthens the public record and withholds what would compromise sovereignty or security.

---

## Article 2 — Data Classification

### 2.1 — Classification Levels

| Level | Label | Description | Access |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 1 | **PUBLIC** | Research papers, public charters, EffectTrace outputs, truth-status displays | Unrestricted |
| 2 | **OPEN-INTERNAL** | Architecture documentation, protocol specifications, framework descriptions | Enterprise contributors |
| 3 | **RESTRICTED** | Proprietary source code, internal doctrine names, implementation details | Authorized personnel only |
| 4 | **SOVEREIGN** | Encrypted personal data, memory vaults, operator credentials | Data subject only |

### 2.2 — Default Classification

- All items in the `papers/` directory: PUBLIC
- All items in the `charters/` directory: PUBLIC
- All items in the `governance/` directory: PUBLIC
- Source code and implementations: RESTRICTED
- User/operator data processed by systems: SOVEREIGN

---

## Article 3 — Open Data Commitments

### 3.1 — What the Enterprise Publishes Openly

| Category | Commitment |
|:---|:---|
| **Research** | All papers in the research series are published as prior art |
| **Governance Intelligence** | EffectTrace outputs — truth status, risk labels, effect traces — are public |
| **Architectural Specifications** | Charters describing system architecture are public |
| **Governance Documents** | All procedural instruments (this policy included) are public |
| **Standards** | Protocol specifications and interface standards are public |

### 3.2 — What the Enterprise Does Not Publish

| Category | Rationale |
|:---|:---|
| **Source code** | Proprietary IP (Master Charter, Article IX) |
| **Internal model weights** | Security and sovereignty |
| **Operator data** | Sovereignty of data subjects |
| **Security configurations** | System integrity |
| **Internal doctrine names** | Builder authorization required (Governing Principle P7) |

---

## Article 4 — Data Formats and Accessibility

4.1. Public data shall be published in open, non-proprietary formats (Markdown, CSV, JSON, PDF).

4.2. Machine-readable formats are preferred where feasible.

4.3. All public documents include clear metadata: author, date, classification, and version history.

4.4. The enterprise maintains public registers (e.g., `AI_Protocols_Register.csv`, `Architectural_Laws_Register.csv`) in machine-readable format.

---

## Article 5 — Data Governance

### 5.1 — Custodianship

The Primary Builder is the ultimate data custodian. Day-to-day data governance is delegated to the Committee of Standards.

### 5.2 — Retention

- Public data: Retained indefinitely (worldline only moves forward)
- Operational data: Retained per system charter requirements
- Personal/sovereign data: Retained per data subject's sovereign control

### 5.3 — Correction

Public data may be corrected but not deleted. Corrections are additive — the original record and the correction both persist in CHRONO.

---

## Article 6 — Compliance with External Standards

6.1. Where the enterprise operates in jurisdictions with data protection requirements (GDPR, CCPA, etc.), sovereign data handling complies with applicable law.

6.2. Compliance is achieved through architectural sovereignty (data subjects control their own vaults) rather than through organizational policy alone.

---

## Article 7 — Governance of This Policy

7.1. This Policy is amended through the procedures defined in the Rules of Procedure (Article 6).

7.2. This Policy does not expire.

7.3. This Policy takes effect immediately upon publication.
